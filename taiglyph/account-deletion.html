TaiGlyph Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: November 6th, 2026
App: TaiGlyph
Developer: MANUEL CIPRIANO

You can request permanent deletion of your TaiGlyph account and the personal learning data associated with it. You do not need to reinstall TaiGlyph to make a request.

Option 1: Request deletion in TaiGlyph

  1. Sign in to TaiGlyph.
  2. Open the account or profile settings and select the account deletion option. [VERIFY EXACT IN-APP NAVIGATION]
  3. Read the confirmation and follow the steps shown in the app.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to support@eratzinsoftware.com with the subject TaiGlyph Account Deletion Request.

In your message, state that you want your TaiGlyph account and associated personal data deleted. If possible, send the request from the email address associated with your account. If you used Sign in with Apple and hid your email address, mention that; we will help identify a suitable verification method.

Do not send your password, verification codes, or other sensitive credentials. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request. Knowing an email address alone may not be sufficient.

Important: Account deletion is intended to be permanent. You may lose your study plans, saved learning progress, review history, streaks, and achievements. Deleting the app from your phone does not itself delete your TaiGlyph account.

What data is deleted?

After verification and processing, the deletion procedure is intended to remove your TaiGlyph account and associated personal learning records, including:

  • Account profile information and associated authentication account.
  • Weekly learning plans and their selected items.
  • Practice and review progress, study sessions, and learning history.
  • Streaks, achievements, and other account-linked learning records.

Some preferences stored only on your device may remain there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

What data might be retained?

Limited records may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or to document that a deletion request was handled. Provider-managed backups and logs may follow separate retention schedules. We do not retain learning records simply to continue providing the deleted account's learning features.

Processing timeframe: We will review and respond to account deletion requests within 7 calendar days of receiving them. Once we have verified your identity and confirmed your request, we will permanently delete your TaiGlyph account and associated personal data within 30 calendar days. Certain information may be retained for a longer period when required by applicable law, necessary for security purposes, or temporarily stored in backups according to our data retention procedures. If verification is needed, we will explain the next steps when responding to your request.
