About the App

TaiGlyph is a Traditional Mandarin learning app developed by Eratzin Software. Designed for learners interested in Taiwan's language and culture, TaiGlyph provides a structured and personalized way to study Traditional Chinese characters, vocabulary, and pronunciation.

Through personalized weekly study plans, interactive practice sessions, and progress tracking, TaiGlyph helps learners build their Mandarin knowledge at their own pace.

Key Features

  • Personalized Weekly Plans: Create study plans with Chinese characters and vocabulary tailored to your learning goals.
  • Interactive Practice: Reinforce your knowledge through recognition, pronunciation, and meaning exercises.
  • Spaced Repetition: Review previously studied material to strengthen long-term retention.
  • Traditional Chinese Dictionary: Explore characters, vocabulary, pronunciations, definitions, and example sentences.
  • Mandarin Pronunciation: Listen to Chinese pronunciations and learn with Pinyin and Zhuyin.
  • Learning Progress: Track completed study sessions, vocabulary mastery, learning streaks, and achievements.
  • TOCFL Vocabulary: Explore vocabulary associated with the Test of Chinese as a Foreign Language.

Learning Approach

TaiGlyph encourages consistent learning through manageable study goals, regular practice, and vocabulary review. Learners can organize their weekly activities and monitor their progress as they develop their Traditional Mandarin skills.

Privacy & Data Protection

TaiGlyph respects user privacy and provides account management features, including the ability to request permanent account deletion.

Learning progress and account information are managed using Firebase services. For more information about how personal data is handled, please review our Privacy Policy.
