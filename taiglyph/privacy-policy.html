TaiGlyph Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6th, 2026
App: TaiGlyph
Developer: MANUEL CIPRIANO

TaiGlyph ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile application designed to help people learn Traditional Mandarin Chinese, with a focus on Taiwan. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and process when you use TaiGlyph, why we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, TaiGlyph may process:

  • Account information: your email address, display name (when provided), unique account identifier, and sign-in provider information. Password-based authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication; we do not need to store your plaintext password in our application database.
  • Learning information: weekly study plans, selected characters and vocabulary, answers and practice results, mastery and review progress, study sessions, learning history, streaks, and achievements.
  • Preferences: app settings, such as theme, sound, pronunciation, and notification preferences.
  • Technical information: limited device, application, authentication, and service-related information processed by our infrastructure providers to operate and secure the app.
  • Information you provide when contacting us: the details of a support or privacy inquiry.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Save and synchronize study plans, progress, and learning history.
  • Provide practice, review, dictionary, pronunciation, and achievement features.
  • Remember preferences and deliver study reminders when enabled.
  • Maintain app functionality, prevent misuse, investigate technical issues, and respond to support requests.
  • Process account deletion requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Authentication and Third-Party Services

TaiGlyph uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions, to support accounts and app functionality. When you choose a third-party sign-in option, such as Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple (where available), the relevant provider also processes information according to its own privacy practices.

For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security, Google Privacy Policy, and Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications and Pronunciation

If you enable study reminders, TaiGlyph may use your device's notification functionality to display reminders. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings. TaiGlyph also offers pronunciation functionality using device text-to-speech capabilities; the processing of speech requests can depend on your device, operating system, and selected voice engine.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share or make information available to service providers as needed to operate TaiGlyph, including authentication, hosting, database, and cloud processing providers. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a lawful business transition, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including access controls and the security features of our cloud service providers. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Information may be processed in locations where our service providers operate.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account and learning information for as long as needed to provide TaiGlyph and for other legitimate purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable law. You may request account deletion using the deletion feature available in the app, where supported, or by contacting us at the email address below.

When an account deletion request is processed, we delete or de-identify associated personal information according to our deletion procedures, except where retention is required or permitted for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes. Backups and provider-managed records may be removed on separate schedules.

Account deletion information: See the TaiGlyph Account Deletion page.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage certain app preferences and device permissions, and you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. Additional rights may apply depending on your location. We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

9. Children's Privacy

TaiGlyph is an educational app. Its intended audience and any age-related restrictions should be reflected in the app's store listing and onboarding. If we learn that personal information was collected from a child in a manner that requires parental consent or other protections under applicable law, we will take appropriate action. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in TaiGlyph, our service providers, or legal requirements. We will publish the revised policy with an updated effective date and provide additional notice when required.
